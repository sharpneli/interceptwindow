## Anchors

### Prometheus Ring – Vadim Sadovski

File: `prometheus-ring-vadim-sadovski.png` (local only)

> "Prometheus Ring, a 1200 km diameter habitat in Saturn's orbit, spinning to generate 1g, powered by nuclear reactors and stabilized by plasma thrusters."

A spin habitat hangs above Saturn's rings. It casts a hard shadow on the ring plane, and two small moons sit as crescents against the planet's night side.

What it says about the look:

- **Scale through emptiness.** The ring is small in the frame, although its stated size is huge. Most of the image is planet, ring and black sky. Human works are small against natural ones.
- **One hard light source.** Sunlight at Saturn is weak but unfiltered. Edges are crisp, shadows are black, and there is no fill light or glow.
- **Muted, near-monochrome palette.** Greys, ring-ice white and a faint warm tone on the cloud tops. Colour is rare, so a small blue thruster glint reads at once.
- **Engineering, not spectacle.** The structure looks built. Panels and seams are visible, and nothing flares, beams or sparkles.
- **Quiet.** No combat and no action. Danger, when it comes, should feel like an intrusion into this stillness.
