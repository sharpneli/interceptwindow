Input grammar and view rules

Fleetwright's click grammar on a solar-system map. No free camera; the view is always centred on something.
Mockup v0.2 · Nov 8, 2026
Mousesame on every screen
LMBSelect. An object on the map, a row in the outliner, a cell on a plot. Selecting opens the object's window as a preview (italic title), which the next selection replaces. Empty space clears the selection and closes the preview. Selecting never moves the camera.
MMBCentre on what is under the cursor. The one-button camera move: middle-click a planet, fleet or track, then wheel in. The camera glides there (0.7 s) and picks a sensible range. Selection and windows are left alone, so you can look somewhere without losing what you were working on.
LMBLMB or FCentre, alternatives. Double-click also centres. F centres on the current selection. Middle-drag still orbits the camera; under 4 px of movement it counts as a click.
RMBAct. With an own group selected, opens its order menu for the thing under the cursor, default order first and in bold ("Plan transfer to Ceres…" opens the planner window). With nothing of ours selected: centre, or open a sticky window. On a porkchop: commit that solution.
TRIWArm an order (transfer, rendezvous, intercept, shadow) from the command strip or a key. An amber banner shows it is armed. Then LMB on a valid target confirms it, and RMB or Esc cancels. An invalid target leaves the order armed and says why. No modal dialogs.
Drag, any buttonOrbit the camera around the focus. A movement of 4 px or more makes it a drag, not a click. There is no panning.
WheelZoom toward the focus, logarithmic: 0.14 decade per notch, 0.04 with Ctrl. Eased, never stepped.
BreadcrumbSun › Earth › Moon › TF Kestrel : click a crumb to centre on it. This is how you climb out.
Zoom rulerClick to jump to a range. The bands are marked on it.
Esc DelEsc cancels an armed order, then closes the top window. Delete closes every window that is not sticky (as in Transport Tycoon).
Space Home F2 F3 LPause or 1 d/s · centre on the Sun · outliner · transfer planner · log.
Never
  • The camera never moves on its own: no jump to an event, no follow-cam toggle (Fleetwright decision 15). Events go to the log; the interest table decides whether the clock slows.
  • No free-floating camera position. Focus is an object, and the frame moves with it.
  • The zoom never changes the sim. Bands change only how things are drawn.
  • No popping. Moons, labels and small bodies fade in with their size on screen. "No hard cutoffs" holds in the UI too.
  • No order confirmations except the armed state and the default-first menu.
  • No permanent side panels. Information lives in windows the player opened, and Delete clears them.

Windowsthe map is the screen
  • The map fills the screen. Chrome is four translucent plates: polity and launchers, centre breadcrumb, clock, and a one-line report ticker. Orders appear at the bottom only while one of our groups is selected.
  • Everything else is a floating window: group, station, track, body, transfer planner, outliner, log. Drag by the title bar; double-click it to roll the window up.
  • Group and station windows open on a live vignette, like a Transport Tycoon vehicle window, with a button column and a status bar that centres the map when clicked. The vignette shows the ships, so the map never needs to zoom that close.
  • The vignette is honest: own assets are live (formation compressed, real spread stated), foreign ones show registry imagery, and tracks show only the sensor's blob.
  • The transfer planner is a window. Its selected solution is drawn on the map as a dashed preview arc, with ghost markers for departure and arrival.
  • Only the ship designer and similar tools take the full screen.
